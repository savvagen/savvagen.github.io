02 — What I do

Services & Expertise

Engagements I have delivered repeatedly — as an individual contributor, as a technical lead, and as the person who leaves the practice running after they go.

Performance Strategy & NFRs

Gathering SLAs/SLOs from product owners, translating them into measurable non-functional requirements, and turning those into test plans and per-component strategies.

Load Testing Platforms

Distributed, cloud-native load-generation platforms on Kubernetes, EKS and AKS with K6, Gatling, Locust, Artillery and JMeter — built from scratch or rescued from an unmaintained state.

Observability & Monitoring

Prometheus/Thanos, Grafana (Loki, Tempo), InfluxDB, Datadog, New Relic, Elasticsearch/Kibana and OpenTelemetry — dashboards and alerting that make regressions obvious.

Bottleneck Analysis & Profiling

End-to-end troubleshooting across services, databases and infrastructure: JVM profiling with VisualVM and JProfiler, memory-leak hunting, query and capacity analysis.

CI/CD & Shift-Left Performance

Performance gates inside GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins and CircleCI, with ephemeral load agents, automated reporting and trend baselines instead of one-off manual runs.

Test Automation Frameworks

API, integration and E2E frameworks in TypeScript, Java, Kotlin, Python and C# — Playwright, Selenide, RestAssured, Pytest, JUnit5 — with Allure reporting and coverage insight.

AI-Assisted Engineering Workflows

Introducing Copilot, Claude, Codex, Junie and JetBrains AI Assistant into real delivery: generation conventions, custom agent skills and commands, and guardrails teams actually follow.

Team Leadership & Enablement

Hiring, interviewing, onboarding and mentoring performance engineers; knowledge sharing with QA, SRE and development teams; establishing engineering standards across products.
